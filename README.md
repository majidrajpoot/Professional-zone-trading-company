## How to Choose the Right LED Screen

Every LED display project has unique requirements. Consider the following factors before selecting a solution.

- **Screen Size:** Match display dimensions to the venue and audience.
- **Pixel Pitch:** Choose suitable image detail for the intended viewing distance.
- **Brightness:** Consider indoor lighting or outdoor sunlight exposure.
- **Installation:** Evaluate mounting, structural support, ventilation, and power requirements.
- **Content Compatibility:** Confirm compatibility with media players and control systems.
- **Maintenance:** Consider servicing access and long-term operating requirements.
- **Budget:** Evaluate equipment, installation, and maintenance costs.

Choosing an appropriate configuration helps align the display with the project's objectives, venue, and budget.
